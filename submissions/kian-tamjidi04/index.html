Whackschach

Each call to whack() becomes an inkblot. Every letter steers a pen: its ASCII code sets how far the pen turns and how far it moves. Each word ends in a drop of ink sized by its length. The result is mirrored down the middle, and an examiner reports what they see.

Letter tape

    
How the pen moved. Each letter turns it by (code − 109.5) × 6 degrees, so a curls hard left and z hard right, and steps it forward by 6 + 2 × (code mod 7). Before each word the heading swings by the word's ASCII sum mod 360. The tape is the instructions, one tile per letter in reading order. The blot is the result: the same moves in bleeding ink, each word ending in a splat, then mirrored so overlapping strokes merge into one shape.
